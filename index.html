Built to grow, whichever she picks

  • One product file. Every sticker, price, collection and description lives in shared/data/catalog.js. Adding a sticker is one new entry; it appears on every page and filter.
  • Her real designs, both languages. Each bilingual design is one product with an English and a Español version; shoppers pick one on the product page.
  • Bilingual by design. Every label switches between English and Español, and the choice is remembered.
  • Checkout is one setting. Paste a Stripe Payment Link, Shopify or Snipcart URL into checkoutUrl at launch.
  • Sample content. Prices, sizes, finishes and the Starter Pack are placeholders until the client confirms them.
