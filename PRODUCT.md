# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. Products live in a single data file so adding a sticker is one new entry. Client-side cart (persisted per browser); checkout is a hand-off point to be wired later to Stripe Payment Links, Shopify Buy Button, or Snipcart. Hosting target: any static host (Netlify / Vercel).

## Users

Women who train (gym, lifting, running, classes) and who live in both English and Spanish. They buy stickers to decorate water bottles, laptops, planners, gym gear and journals, as gifts for gym friends, or as a small reward for themselves. They arrive mostly from Instagram on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Fortabella is a Latina-owned, bilingual sticker company for women who love fitness. The shop sells inspiring stickers that pair strength with beauty. Success: a visitor from Instagram finds a sticker that sounds like her, in her language(s), and checks out in a few taps.

## Positioning

Stickers that speak both of her languages and treat strength as a form of beauty. Motto: "Strength is beauty! / La Fortaleza es belleza!"

## Operating Context

Starting out with a small launch catalog. The site must scale from a handful of stickers to collections, packs, bundles and categories without a redesign. Instagram: @fortabellastickers. Domain: fortabellashop.com (not live yet).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: full EN/ES toggle for UI and product copy, plus bilingual voice in headlines and taglines. Sticker phrases stay in their original language.
- Catalog, product detail, cart. Checkout provider undecided.
- Undecided: prices, shipping rates, checkout provider, product names.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Fortabella. Latina-owned. Bilingual.
- Motto: "Strength is beauty! / La Fortaleza es belleza!"
- Voice: empowering, warm, feminine, fitness-positive.
- Logo (supplied 2026-11-25, `Logo_transparent.tiff`): Old English blackletter "Fortabella" in cream with a gilded gold bevel and black outline, crimson wild roses with gold centres, olive leaves and gold filigree; tagline "STRENGTH IS BEAUTY ◆ LA FORTALEZA ES BELLEZA" in bold geometric caps with a gold diamond. Every demo is built around it.
- Client direction (2026-11-25): clean, minimal, elegant and very feminine; simple navigation for picking stickers; expandable as the catalog grows.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real sticker designs supplied 2026-11-25 (Lifting and Lashes / Pesas y Pestañas, Cafecito y Kettlebells, I Love Pets and Heavy Sets / Patitas y Pesas, I Love Lipstick and Lifting / Amo el Labial y Levantar Pesas, plus a wide wordmark); all are in the demos.
- No prices, reviews, testimonials, customer counts or press exist. Do not fabricate them; any price in the build is a placeholder flagged in the data file.

## Product Principles

1. Bilingual is the product, not a translation layer.
2. Strength and femininity are one idea, never opposites.
3. Growth without redesign: everything catalog-shaped comes from data.
4. Phone-first: the Instagram visitor is the primary visitor.
